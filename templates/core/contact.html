Frequently Asked Questions

How do I organize an event through EVENZA?

To organize an event, simply contact us using the form above or visit our department. Our student team will guide you through the entire process, from planning to execution.

How far in advance should I book an event?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks in advance for small events, and 4-6 weeks for larger events to ensure proper planning and resource allocation.

Can I participate in events organized by other departments?

Yes! Most events organized through EVENZA are open to all students across departments. Check our events page for upcoming opportunities.

What types of events can EVENZA help organize?

We specialize in academic events (workshops, seminars), tech events (hackathons, coding competitions), cultural events, and networking sessions for students.

How can I join the EVENZA team?

We recruit new team members at the beginning of each semester. Contact us expressing your interest, and we'll notify you about the next recruitment drive.
