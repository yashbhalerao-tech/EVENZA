Our Journey

GPK College • 5th Semester Project

EVENZA began as our Industrial Training project for the 5th semester of our Computer Science diploma at GPK College. As part of our Python Web Development using Django course, we were challenged to create a meaningful web application that solves a real-world problem.

As Computer Science students, we experienced firsthand how challenging it was to manage department events using scattered spreadsheets, group chats, and paper sign-ups. The inefficiency of these methods often led to miscommunication and missed opportunities.

Frustrated by these limitations, we decided to create a dedicated platform that would streamline event management for academic departments. What started as a course requirement quickly evolved into a passion project as we realized the potential impact it could have on student organizations.

Over the course of our Industrial Training, our team of four CS students designed, developed, and deployed EVENZA using Django, Python, and modern web technologies. We conducted user research with fellow students and faculty to ensure the platform met real needs.

Today, EVENZA stands as a testament to what student developers can achieve when they combine technical skills with a desire to solve real-world problems in their academic community.

Built with Django & Python

Our Mission & Vision


Our Mission

To empower academic departments with a streamlined event management platform that enhances student engagement and simplifies the organization process for student leaders.

Our Vision

To become the standard event management solution for academic departments, fostering stronger campus communities through better organized events.

Project Goals

Demonstrate our Django skills, solve a real campus problem, and create a platform that can continue to serve students after our Industrial Training.
